Who we are

About Us

Suryansh Kala & Sanskriti Foundation is a cultural initiative dedicated to celebrating and promoting the diverse artistic traditions of India. Through cultural events, performances and community participation, our platform aims to connect people with India’s rich artistic heritage.

Our vision

Preserve the living spirit of Indian culture

We envision a future where art, heritage and tradition remain vibrant, accessible and relevant to every generation.

Our mission

Create cultural bridges

We aim to encourage learning, participation and appreciation through events, performances and immersive cultural experiences.

What we value

Our cultural values

Rooted in respect, creativity and continuity, our values support meaningful engagement with Indian heritage.

Authenticity

We honour classical forms and regional traditions in their original context and expression.

Innovation

We bring heritage into contemporary conversation through fresh storytelling and inclusive participation.

Community

We strengthen cultural belonging by inviting audiences to celebrate art together.

What we do

Curating experiences that connect people to heritage

Through curated performances, workshops, festivals and cultural showcases, we help audiences experience the beauty of Indian traditions in vivid, engaging ways.

  • Cultural festivals and live performances
  • Educational workshops on music, dance and craft
  • Community engagement and heritage storytelling
Our commitment
Heritage

We are committed to keeping India’s artistic heritage alive through celebration, participation and preservation.
