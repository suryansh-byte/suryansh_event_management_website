Why it matters

Cultural heritage is a living, breathing legacy.

Indian traditions are not only rooted in the past, but continue to inspire contemporary creativity, identity and national pride. Preserving them means sustaining memory, craftsmanship and community connection across generations.

  • Protect artistic disciplines that carry centuries of wisdom.
  • Encourage youth participation in cultural learning and performance.
  • Celebrate India’s diversity through shared celebration and appreciation.
